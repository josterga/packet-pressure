Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-09  ·  packetpressure.com

What we collect

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous gameplay telemetry. This includes: game configuration (player count, round count), round and game outcomes, card types played, and gameplay events (help opened, hints toggled). No personally identifiable information is collected. No account is required to play.

This site also stores your theme preference and hints preference in your browser's localStorage. These values never leave your device.

Why we collect it

Gameplay telemetry is used to understand how the game is played — which cards are most common, how long games last, how often the game is completed — so we can improve the rules and balance.

Your consent

Analytics are only loaded after you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Decline, no analytics data is sent. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site.

Third parties

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under their Privacy Policy. No data is sold or shared with any other party.

Data retention

GA4 retains event data for 14 months by default. No other data is stored server-side — this is a static site.
